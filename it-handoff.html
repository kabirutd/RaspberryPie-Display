Message to send to IT

Copy the message below into your IT ticket or email and attach or link these documents. This document does not send anything automatically.

Subject: Set up warehouse TV / Raspberry Pi for continuous Power BI reports

Hi IT team,

We would like the warehouse TV to display our OTD Dashboard and Warehouse Management Power BI reports continuously. I understand another monitor already has a Raspberry Pi setup. Please check whether we can reuse that supported approach for this TV.

Please handle the TV/Pi installation, network connection, automatic full-screen startup, an approved way for me to configure refresh/rotation, prevention of screen sleep, and recovery after a reboot or network interruption.

Please also coordinate secure Power BI access with the Power BI/report owner and identity team. We need an approved display account or player, the correct viewing permissions and licensing, and a documented answer on session expiration. Please do not use public Publish to web links for our private warehouse data.

I am the report developer and will own report configuration, display refresh/rotation, and Power BI source/model refresh settings within my permissions. I will provide the report links, required pages/filters, and operating hours. Please give me the approved player controls or Pi access needed to implement and maintain that mechanism. As a starting proposal, show each report for 60 seconds and refresh the display every 5 minutes; please confirm the platform supports this. I will configure the timing and document source-data freshness separately.

If a standard browser session will require periodic sign-in, please explain how often it may happen, who will restore access, and whether a supported signage or embedded solution is needed for unattended operation.

Before handoff, please demonstrate startup, network recovery, and power/reboot recovery. We will jointly verify my refresh/rotation configuration and current report data. Please give me the approved steps for changing report links, restoring full screen, restarting safely, and contacting support. If another team owns an item, please identify and coordinate that owner.

Thank you.

Your inputs to IT

You do not need to solve the identity or licensing design yourself. If you own report-sharing permissions, you can help grant the approved identity access; otherwise the report owner does it.
